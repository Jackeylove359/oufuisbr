Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I subscribe to a product?

Browse our Products page, click any product to view details, then click "Add to Cart". Visit your cart to review, apply any coupons, and place your order. You'll need to log in (use admin/admin123 for the demo) to checkout.

Which countries does Infomedia support?

Infomedia serves customers in 186 countries with localized parts data, multi-currency billing, and regional language support. Contact our sales team to confirm availability in your region.

Can I change or cancel my subscription?

Yes. Subscriptions are billed monthly and can be upgraded, downgraded, or cancelled at any time from your account portal. Changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

Do you offer training and onboarding?

Absolutely. Every subscription includes guided onboarding, video tutorials, and access to our knowledge base. Enterprise plans include dedicated training sessions with a customer success manager.
