About Infomedia

Three decades of driving the automotive aftermarket forward

Our Story

Founded in 1987 and headquartered in Sydney, Australia, Infomedia began with a simple mission: to digitize the automotive parts catalog. What started as a CD-ROM-based parts lookup tool has grown into a global SaaS platform powering the entire automotive aftermarket.

Today, Infomedia is an ASX-listed company (ASX: IFM) serving over 250,000 users across 186 countries. Our software supports 50+ automotive brands — from mass-market manufacturers to luxury marques — with parts catalogs, ecommerce platforms, service management tools, data analytics, and AI-powered assistants.

We believe the future of the automotive aftermarket is intelligent, connected, and customer-centric. That's why we continue to invest in AI, predictive analytics, and seamless digital experiences that help our customers sell more, serve better, and grow faster.
